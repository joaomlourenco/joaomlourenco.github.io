Yesterday I joined the [GREAT MBA](https://greatmba.fct.unl.pt), Europe's MBA on Critical Raw Materials, as a student.  It is a business-driven executive MBA, offered by NOVA FCT with the University of Miskolc and the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, and initiated and funded by EIT RawMaterials.  It is going to be a long, two-year adventure.
