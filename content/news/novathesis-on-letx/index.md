---
title: "novathesis now opens in letx"
date: 2026-11-14
---

The [novathesis](https://novathesis.org) template now compiles for free on [letx](https://letx.app/templates/universities/novathesis/): the first way into the template that is both free and one click away. The only step left to you is to name your school in `0-Config/1_novathesis.tex`. Read the full post on the [novathesis blog](https://novathesis.org/en/blog/novathesis-on-letx).
