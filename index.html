About me

I am an Associate Professor at the Computer Science Department of NOVA School of Science and Technology (NOVA FCT), NOVA University Lisbon. Since 2023 I am also Co-Director of UNINOVA — Institute for the Development of New Technologies, a non-profit R&D institute. Since 2019 I am also an Adjunct Professor at Ontario Tech University, Canada.

I received my PhD from NOVA University Lisbon in 2004, with a thesis on debugging of distributed programs, and I am a founding member of the NOVA LINCS Research Center. My research focuses on in-memory data management for parallel and large-scale computing systems, verification, testing and debugging of concurrent programs, and edge/fog computing. I am co-author of more than 50 articles and papers, and I have been a visiting researcher at IBM Research Haifa (2011), University of São Paulo at São Carlos (2014), and Brno University of Technology (2015).

I am also a regular contributor to the LaTeX community, and the author of the popular novathesis LaTeX thesis template.

Projects

Principal Investigator

  • HiPSTr — High Performance Software Transactions Jun 2018 – May 2022
    LISBOA-01-0145-FEDER-032456 / PTDC/CCI-COM/32456/2017 PI: João Lourenço (FCT-NOVA)
    This project addresses the dilemma of improving the efficiency of resilient systems while keeping the software systems correct and easy to develop and use. The project proposes a novel concurrency control protocol for replicated systems, exploiting some new hardware features, and a new transactional API, promoting the concurrent execution of operations in all replicas and reducing the conflicts between those operations. The project’s results have the potential to improve the resilience, and reduce the energy consumption, of computer systems.
    National Research Project co-funded by FEDER funds through the LISBOA 2020 Programme and by National funds through FCT/MCTES, 231 K€.
